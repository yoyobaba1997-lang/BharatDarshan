# OTT Platform Coverage

BharatDarshan is a multi-platform metadata discovery catalogue. The registry is a coverage target, not a claim that all platform catalogues are currently indexed or that every title is available in every region.

Initial platform targets include Netflix, Prime Video, JioHotstar, Sony LIV, ZEE5, Amazon MX Player, aha, hoichoi, Sun NXT, ManoramaMAX, Chaupal, ShemarooMe, Eros Now, Lionsgate Play, Planet Marathi OTT, KLiKK, Simply South, Rakuten Viki, iQIYI, Crunchyroll, Apple TV, Discovery+, Curiosity Stream, Tubi, Plex, and official YouTube channels.

Metadata can include platform identifiers and region-specific provider availability, with availability types (subscription, free, rent, buy, or unknown). BharatDarshan does not host, redistribute, or link to unauthorized streams. Platform availability changes by country and over time; provider data must be sourced from authorized/public metadata and refreshed.

The app's platform shelves are designed to be independent of the platform's logo or branding. Use neutral labels and original BharatDarshan UI.
